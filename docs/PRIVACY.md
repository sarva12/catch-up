# Catch Up privacy policy

Effective: August 18, 2026

Catch Up is designed to collect as little information as possible.

## Information stored on the iPhone

Your alarm preference, selected topics, completed story identifiers, streak, and Screen Time app selection are stored locally. The Screen Time selection is represented by opaque Apple-provided tokens; Catch Up does not learn the identities of the selected apps from those tokens.

## Information sent to the news service

When live news is enabled, the app sends only the selected topic names and requested number of stories to the configured Catch Up backend. It does not send your streak, reading history, selected Screen Time apps, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or other profile information.

The hosting provider and upstream news provider may process ordinary network information, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the service under their own terms. The reference backend does not create user accounts or intentionally persist IP addresses.

## Tracking and advertising

Catch Up contains no advertising SDK and does not track people across apps or websites.

## Sources

News summaries link to third-party publishers. Opening a source leaves Catch Up and is governed by that publisher's privacy policy.

## Data deletion

Deleting Catch Up removes its locally stored preferences and reading history. If you operate the reference backend without adding analytics or accounts, it holds no user profile to delete.
